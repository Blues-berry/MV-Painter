\paragraph{Revised calibration and full twenty-object baking.}
The original discontinuous-UV-ramp gate failure is preserved. GT-only diagnostics on all20 fixed inputs reproduce native masks and constant colors, and validate continuous nonlinear world-coordinate colors at the original mean/99th-percentile error bounds of0.03/0.15. An independent two-island analytic fixture agrees to approximately $1.2\times10^{-7}$ and a deliberate image flip is detected. For the mouse and Hair Dryer, all high-error ramp texels on common coverage have a flagged bilinear sampling footprint across a UV jump; errors decrease at higher diagnostic resolutions. This does not assert artifact-free seams: raw UV aliasing and unfilled coverage remain deployment limitations. The revised calibration is post hoc, after15 original object outcomes. Projection, masks, cameras, source/texture resolutions, material and geometry remain unchanged. All375 original GLB hashes and metric records reproduce exactly. No object or method is discarded by quality direction.
The complete evaluation contains740 unique bakes:12 existing methods by3 seeds by20 objects plus20 GT self-bakes, with11 unseen cameras per bake. All foreground remains the reported quality region. Coverage is diagnostic; GT-bake error is not subtracted. The retained correction family uses its original Holm16, while the seven schedule-pair/four-metric diagnostic family uses Holm84.
