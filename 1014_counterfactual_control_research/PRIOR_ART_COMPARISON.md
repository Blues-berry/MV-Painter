# Prior-Art and Novelty Comparison

## Scope and conclusion

This review checks whether a view-wise transfer of geometry-residual scale, selected from a legal reference-image error, is distinct from known online guidance selection, scheduled conditioning scales, geometry/reference conflict handling, and UV-space multi-view feedback. The mechanism has not been established as a method. The literature comparison is therefore a novelty boundary, not a claim that any cited method is mathematically identical.

**Preliminary novelty assessment: weak.** A positive result could support a new empirical control signal for this particular adapter and bake protocol. It would not by itself establish a new general algorithm. If the operational rule is “generate B+ and B−, score both against the reference, choose the lower score,” it is two-candidate test-time selection, with two full generations and no novel search procedure.

## Finite-difference and candidate-selection interpretation

B+ and B− are a two-sided finite-difference probe of a single scalar transfer direction at fixed six-view applied-scale sum. That is a useful causal test of this model's response. It is not, alone, a new optimizer or controller. The feedback rule only becomes more than a two-candidate selection heuristic if it predicts a useful direction before paying for both complete generations, or if it provides a cheaper update with repeatable baked-quality gains over B0 and fixed-direction policies.

The online-feedback CFG work demonstrates a sharper compute comparison: it evaluates several candidate guidance scales using one denoiser prediction at a step. Our design currently requires separate trajectories for B+ and B−. Thus the relevant comparison is not only quality direction, but incremental quality per additional full generation. We will report B0 (one call), fixed B+ or B− (one call), and source-error-selected B+/B− (two calls); the latter is explicitly labeled a two-call selection baseline.

## Prior-art judgment for R2.1

Even if the paired bake test finds a reliable local effect, the method claim must remain narrow: a reference-error action-response for per-view adapter residual transfer on this GeoTex generator and fixed bake pipeline. To justify formal development, the selected action must beat B0 and fixed directions on multiple held-out objects, show no systematic loss elsewhere, and pay for its extra inference. It must also be distinguished from online scale selection, scheduled ControlNet strength, RomanTex geometry/reference conflict handling, and UV-space multi-view feedback. If it only picks between two completed candidates, classify it as simple test-time selection and do not present it as an independent algorithmic contribution.
