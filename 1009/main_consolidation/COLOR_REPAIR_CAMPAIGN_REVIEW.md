## Phase B: four-object chroma sensitivity

A pre-existing Phase B process ran from 05:27:43 to 05:36:36 UTC on one RTX 5090. This task did not start or modify it. Its run manifest agrees with the runtime, result CSV, and condition-trace hashes. The completed probe contains 64 generation calls and 384 per-view result rows: 16 conditions × six target views × four locked development objects. Rows are finite; source/target view identity and geometry features match within object, and initial latent hashes are identical. Two failure-enriched cases fail the cache-recompute identity control (maximum absolute differences 2.847 and 4.047); the two Fresh C development cases pass.

The global-embedding-only arm passes the locked directional-response threshold for `a*` in both directions on all four objects, including the source and all five other views. The VAE-only arm passes neither channel. The embedding-only `b*` arm passes the per-object threshold for two of four objects, below the locked three-of-four criterion. The joint arm passes `a*`, but it is not a channel-only control. These results show selected-example sensitivity to appearance conditioning; they do not identify the original color-cast cause.

## Phase B follow-up: source/cache audit and reference conditions

A later input-path audit records the exact transformed condition, processor/encoder configuration, cache identities, and source-view selection. In the frozen Fresh B 150-object input-only audit, recomputing the global embedding from the exact current transformed condition matched the stored cache within 0.03 for 0/150 inputs; on the 98 selected-view-014 objects, cached features were closer to raw view 000 than selected raw view 014 for 84/98. These are input/cache correspondences, not proof that the mismatch caused the color artifacts.

The four-object reference comparison gives No Adapter a mean unseen-view ΔCIEDE2000 penalty of +20.067 versus GFL, but geometry/lightness also changes substantially, so this does not isolate a pure adapter color effect. LLH is mixed across objects (+2.564 mean ΔCIEDE2000; mean L* SSIM 0.576). Recomputing the global embedding moved the two Fig. 4 failure outputs in opposite GT-relative directions (−0.370 and +0.284 ΔCIEDE2000); the two Fresh C development outputs were pixel-identical to GFL. This shows that cache refresh can change failure outputs without providing a consistent correction. The global appearance path is a causal color-control channel in the selected development probe, while the full color-cast cause remains unresolved.

A subsequent CPU-only join found that the frozen Fresh B condition inputs match the prior-generation condition identities for 150/150 UIDs, while the stored embeddings match that prior generation for 0/150. On the same four development objects, the pre-locked magenta-residual rule measured GFL means of 1.24% on the source views and 1.34% on unseen views. Robust, unregistered Lab distributions placed the source condition close to source GT (median ΔE 0.17–0.48 across the four objects), while the GFL source-tile distributions differed more (median ΔE 8.83–16.20). The object-level readout also reports GFL source-view median ΔE 18.20–21.56 and unseen-view mean ΔE 19.05–24.08 against GT. These selected-object distribution readouts do not isolate the responsible stage or establish perceived color fidelity; the thresholded magenta fraction also does not measure every color mismatch.

`A_SOURCE_VIEW_COLOR_AUDIT.md`, the generated reference comparison, per-view color readout, source statistics, and Fresh B input reconstruction are local-only diagnostics. No Fresh B repair metrics or holdout prediction pixels were opened. These results did not change the manuscript or its claim ceiling.

## Phase C: frozen C1 source-chroma anchor candidate

The retained worktree then applied its prelocked, source-conditioned Lab chroma anchor to the same four development objects and kept each candidate in a separate PNG. GT colors were used only after candidate generation. The numeric object-level gate passed: mean five-view CIEDE2000 change −1.560 (95% paired object-bootstrap interval [−2.262, −0.630]), mean FG-PSNR change +0.181 dB, mean LPIPS change −0.00186, and no material mean L*-SSIM loss. With only four selected development objects, these descriptive intervals are not independent validation.

The frozen manual gate failed. In both failure-enriched Fig. 4 cases, visual review found no clear repair; one candidate introduced a cool cyan/blue rim while substantial source-to-target color mismatch remained. The normal-color and high-texture cases retained detail. The protocol therefore stops before Fresh B: no Fresh B candidate pixels or repair metrics were opened, no holdout validation was run, and no general repair claim is supported.
