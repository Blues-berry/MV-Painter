# D — Fix-candidate gate

**Status: no candidate qualified; Phase D is skipped.**

The gate required a specific, independently supported intervention point before any repair trial. The audit found consistent object/condition/embedding identities and no verified color-space or preprocessing bug. GFL lowers average Fresh C CIEDE2000 versus No Adapter, although a* and b* residual magnitudes move in the opposite direction and visible errors remain in selected cases. LLH improves average CIEDE2000 on Fresh C/B but shows a high-texture failure boundary. The prior VAE round-trip check is too small and differently directed to explain the reviewed generated mismatch by itself.

No single code correction follows from those observations. A different appearance encoder, view-conditioned RGB constraint or schedule would be new method development or result-driven tuning, not repair of an established defect. No Gate threshold, Adapter scale, denoising boundary, GT color correction or new model variant was searched or run.

The decision is recorded as a gate result, not as a failed candidate experiment. Existing controls remain the frozen No Adapter/GFL/LLH images and the prior VAE isolation; they are not relabeled as a repair.
