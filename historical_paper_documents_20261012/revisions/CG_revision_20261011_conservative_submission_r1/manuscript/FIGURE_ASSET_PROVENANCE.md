# Figure provenance — conservative submission r1

The paper's result figures use the existing frozen runs and rights-cleared assets. No model outputs, training runs, or schedules were added for this revision.

Figure 1 is a code-generated vector schematic of the TCAS path. Figure 5 plots every Fresh C C3-minus-Fixed-Low object-level FG-PSNR difference and named paired contrasts. Figure 6 compares two fixed-scale/linear cases with shared view and crop. Figure 7 retains the selected color diagnostic. Figure 8 shows the two metric-selected C3-versus-Fixed-Low cases, Cube 01 (UID `06fa4974f8cd4d4ea792e98fa1521457`) and E5-5-2 (UID `f6979d7ff8e54c4daf00fee46f09a139`). Figure 9 shows calibrated unseen-camera baking; Figure 10 shows the native MV-Adapter comparison.

For Figure 8, candidate objects were limited to five previously rights-cleared Fresh C illustrations. Selection required C3 to improve over Fixed Low on composite FG-PSNR, FG-LPIPS and FG-SSIM, and for displayed view 0 to show the same three metric directions. The frozen evaluator scores each saved 3-by-2 six-view composite once per object; per-view values are a display check only. Both selected cases satisfy the object-level and view-0 rules. Their displayed ROI `(48, 90, 208, 210)` is shared across the Target, Fixed Low and C3 panels and comes from the existing ROI manifest. The target renders are low-variation, so Figure 8 illustrates the image-space metric direction and does not establish texture fidelity or prevalence. Figure 5 and the complete Fresh C tables carry the cohort evidence.

`FIG7_METRIC_FAVORABLE_PROVENANCE.json` records the selected objects, object and view-0 metric differences, common crop, and the PNG-versus-frozen quantization differences. `FIG7_METRIC_FAVORABLE_SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` and `FRESH_C_FIGURE_SOURCE_ROI_MANIFEST.csv` bind source, run, seed, checkpoint, view, crop and file hashes. `FIGURE_ASSET_CREDITS.csv` and Supplementary Table S1 identify the CC BY 4.0 asset creators. PNG quantization is listed only as a saved-image diagnostic; the frozen object-level table is unchanged.

Figure 9 uses the first two rights-cleared baked objects and Figure 10 uses the first two rights-cleared native MV-Adapter objects in their frozen input order. Their source hashes, shared display frames, crop rules and attribution are recorded alongside the figure assets. Figures S2–S8 retain every bake condition for all seven rights-cleared objects, and Figure S9 retains all seven MV-Adapter conditions.
