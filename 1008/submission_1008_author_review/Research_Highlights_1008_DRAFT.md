# Research Highlights — 1008 draft

- TCAS measures requested, applied, and residual scales in an existing geometry-adapter path.
- Fresh C compares paired image-space outputs for 300 held-out objects.
- C3 exceeds GFL by 0.522 dB FG-PSNR; GFH and Generic Linear remain strong comparators.
- Retrospective diagnostics show object- and endpoint-dependent texture and color responses.
- Image-space evidence does not establish baked-texture, seam, or PBR quality.
