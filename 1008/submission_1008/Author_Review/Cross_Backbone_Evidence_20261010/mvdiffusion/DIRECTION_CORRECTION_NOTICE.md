# MVDiffusion direction-correction notice

The original MVDiffusion analysis reports are retained unchanged as historical records. Their lower-is-better endpoints use improvement-coded signs, and the old report's P5 sentence incorrectly says the FG-LPIPS interval favors G-LHL.

Use `1008/audits/MVDIFFUSION_RAW_DIRECTION_CORRECTION_20261010/MVDIFFUSION_RAW_DIRECTION_CORRECTION.md`, its `MVDIFFUSION_RAW_CONTRASTS.csv`, and manuscript Supplementary Table S12 for authoritative raw A-minus-B effects and pointwise bootstrap intervals. In P5, raw G-LLH minus G-LHL FG-LPIPS is −0.001371 [−0.002642,−0.000141], favoring G-LLH. The old report and bootstrap records remain available to preserve provenance; do not use their lower-is-better signs as raw differences.
