# Research Highlights — 1008 draft

- TCAS applies a low–high–low scale profile to an existing geometry adapter.
- Fresh C compares matched outputs on 300 objects under one frozen checkpoint.
- C3 exceeds GFL by 0.522 dB mean FG-PSNR (95% CI 0.404–0.641).
- GFH and Generic Linear provide counter-results to a universal C3 claim.
- Image-space results do not establish 3D material quality or human preference.
