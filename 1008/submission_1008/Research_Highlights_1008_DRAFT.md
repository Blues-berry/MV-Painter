# Research Highlights — 1008 draft

- TCAS applies a three-stage requested-scale profile to an existing geometry adapter.
- Fresh C compares matched rendered outputs for 300 held-out objects.
- C3 exceeds GFL by 0.522 dB mean FG-PSNR (95% CI 0.404–0.641).
- C3 and GFL differ in applied deep/middle scale sums; the result is not timing-only.
- Image-space results do not establish baked-texture, seam, or PBR quality.
