## Frozen interpretation

The paper's contribution is framed as an operationally explicit, task-specific empirical study of an existing geometry-residual control in a multi-view texturing system. It is not presented as a new general scheduler, a dose-independent mechanism, or a universal optimum. Fresh C is the primary matched evidence; separately sourced diagnostics are explicitly marked by cohort, endpoint, and evidence level.
