## Assessment

The recommendation is substantially sound. The current results can support a bounded empirical paper about implementation-aware geometry-control evaluation and the relationship between generated views and baked assets. Repeating every historical audit in the main text weakens the sequence from executed intervention, through strong controls, to asset-level outcome. The best revision is selective compression with the complete historical record retained in the Supplement and reviewer response.

## Scientific limits that remain

The wording changes do not create a new schedule optimization method or independently derive C3. The matched-budget primary does not detect a middle-window advantage; retrospective pairwise results show cohort-specific placement sensitivity without identifying a preferred schedule or residual-dose mechanism. The GeoTex bake effect is small and comes from ten compatibility-selected objects, while substantial error remains against the matched self-bake; GSO20 does not detect the same effect. Native-interface studies use different operators and primary contrasts. The full-v29 comparisons do not isolate TCAS because weights and geometry implementations differ and the protocol is not the official default. Historical main-checkpoint identity and one substituted UID remain unauthenticated.

These are scientific boundaries, not missing V5 packaging work. The response preserves them and does not present LaTeX compilation as evidence of scientific validity.
