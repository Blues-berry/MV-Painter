# Independent C&G-style assessment — V5 candidate

This is an independent editorial assessment of the V5 13-page main text, 71-page Supplement, six-page response and 20-page marked manuscript. It is not a prediction of the editor's or reviewers' decision.

## Scientific contribution

The three contributions form a coherent bounded empirical graphics paper, but they do not have equal evidential strength.

1. **Implementation-aware control characterization is the strongest contribution.** The paper separates requested scale, native-cap-adjusted applied scale and state-dependent residual correction. On 300 held-out objects it shows why C3's gain over Fixed Low is not a timing-only result, compares it with stronger fixed/linear settings, and tests a specified matched-applied-budget contrast. A separate retrospective all-pair family distinguishes Middle-high from Early-high and Late-high within this cohort at equal applied sums; this makes placement sensitivity visible without replacing the non-significant specified primary or establishing a schedule rule. This contributes beyond a single C3 ablation because the executed intervention and the claim identified by each comparator are explicit.
2. **The native-UV bake endpoint gives the paper its direct graphics value.** It connects generated-view measurements to rendered textured meshes at unseen cameras against matched reference self-bakes. The GeoTex pilot finds a small local Late-high LPIPS change alongside a much larger appearance gap; the separate GSO20 protocol does not detect the same benefit. This demonstrates why the image endpoint alone is insufficient, while the small compatibility-selected cohorts limit transfer.
3. **The native-interface studies define transfer boundaries.** Their study-specific primaries do not establish a portable middle-window rule. GSO100 uses the same MV-Adapter backbone as MV-Adapter50, and MVDiffusion scales a different partial operator. These results are useful sensitivity evidence, not three independent backbone replications.

Together, the work is more than one schedule ablation because it triangulates the intervention, the strength and timing alternatives, and the final asset produced by a graphics pipeline. Its publishable claim is an implementation-aware evaluation of control allocation for multi-view texturing. No new scheduling algorithm, general mechanism, selector or optimum is demonstrated. Prior scheduling work means method-novelty risk remains substantial and cannot be removed by stronger prose.

## Narrative, order and redundancy

The revised Introduction moves from the RGB-to-UV graphics target, through the confounding between strength and timing, to image-versus-asset evaluation and the study questions. Its short result preview and contribution paragraph echo some later material, as expected in a research introduction, but no longer inventory full-v29, texture-complexity and every non-significant test. Section 3 defines the executed interface before the cohorts and evaluation units. Results then move from competitive controls and matched-budget allocation to color/appearance diagnosis, the main bake endpoint, native-interface sensitivity and historical context. Discussion interprets four themes: nominal controls, baked appearance, interface/object dependence, and practical implications/limits.

Some endpoint caveats recur where readers need them—especially in the bake section, Discussion and response—but the main argument does not depend on repeated disclaimers. The historical section is now one compact paragraph: it retains the missing authenticated +0.96 dB reproduction, the unknown replacement UID and opposing endpoint directions, then points to S7 for the full 276-slot, preference and FAC record. This makes the sequence more direct while preserving reviewer traceability. The current order is defensible: strong within-interface evidence precedes smaller asset and transfer cohorts, with retrospective history last.

## Limits prose cannot close

The main text now explains the value and limits of existing evidence, but this revision cannot supply:

- a new schedule-selection or optimization method, or a causal residual-dose mechanism;
- an architecture-matched, official-default, complete-system TCAS-only comparison;
- a larger representative bake cohort or a prospective replication of the GeoTex effect;
- validated seam, PBR, or physical-material fidelity measurements;
- the missing historical checkpoint identity, unknown replacement UID, or complete FAC replay record.

Those require new experiments, recovered records, or both. The small bake effects, post-inspection GSO calibration, convenience cohorts, and unmatched full-v29 system remain visible limitations. No inference family or statistical evidence tier is promoted.

## Readiness judgment

**AUTHOR_REVIEW_READY: YES.** The revised narrative, figure decisions, cohort boundaries and limitations are internally consistent and supported by the supplied evidence.

**SUBMISSION_READY: YES, as a bounded empirical C&G manuscript.** The package is complete and its claims are appropriately scoped. This judgment is not based on compilation alone: it reflects the controlled comparison design, explicit distinction between view and asset endpoints, full-cohort data retained in the Supplement, clear visual-selection disclosures, provenance-checked media and marked manuscript, and the explicit limits above. The corresponding author should review the remaining novelty risk before submission. Portal upload has not been performed; readiness is not an acceptance forecast.
