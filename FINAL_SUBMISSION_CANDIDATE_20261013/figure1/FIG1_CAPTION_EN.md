Overview of the TCAS control interface in multi-view diffusion texturing. The frozen pretrained GeoTex-Adapter computes geometry-conditioned residual corrections from normal, depth and foreground-mask features, together with current hidden features. TCAS specifies the historical C3 requested scaling profile (1.25, 2.50, 1.25) over steps 0--16, 17--32 and 33--49. Existing native group-specific caps determine the applied scales before residual multiplication and addition to frozen U-Net features. In the evaluated Fresh C implementation, the deep, middle and shallow caps are 3.0, 3.5 and 0.8; shallow is clipped to 0.8 throughout. Six generated RGB views can subsequently be projected and baked onto a textured mesh. The author-supplied imagery illustrates the workflow and is not used as cohort evidence. OpenAI Codex v0.162.1 assisted SVG export and integration.
