     ARTICLE INFO                                 ABSTRACT

                                                  Geometry-conditioned multi-view diffusion produces images that are baked onto 3D
     Keywords: multi-view diffusion, 3D tex-      meshes, but the interpretation of a geometry-control schedule depends on its imple-
     turing, geometry adapter, residual scal-     mented strength and the evaluated endpoint. We study Timestep-Conditioned Adapter
     ing, timestep conditioning                   Scaling (TCAS) on an existing GeoTex residual path, separating requested scale, native-
                                                  cap-adjusted applied scale and state-dependent residual correction. On 300 held-out
                                                  objects, the historical low–high–low C3 profile gains 0.522 dB foreground PSNR over
                                                  Fixed Low while increasing the applied budget; Fixed High and Generic Linear have
                                                  better means on all four image endpoints. At matched applied-scale sums, the specified
                                                  Middle-high minus average(Early-high,Late-high) contrast does not detect a middle-
                                                  window advantage (−0.0339 dB, 95% interval [−0.1172, +0.0514], p = 0.4324). Actual
                                                  native-UV baking yields a small Late-high foreground LPIPS benefit on ten compati-
                                                  ble GeoTex objects (−0.00265), which is not detected in a separate twenty-object GSO
                                                  study. Native MV-Adapter comparisons likewise do not detect the specified middle-
                                                  window gain. The findings change the interpretation of C3 and identify practical eval-
                                                  uation requirements: competitive controls, matching at the implemented interface, and
                                                  unseen-view assessment of the resulting asset. They establish task-specific allocation
                                                  effects and limits, without a universal schedule or faithful material reproduction.
