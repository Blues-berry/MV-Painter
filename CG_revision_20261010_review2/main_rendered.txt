     ARTICLE INFO                                 ABSTRACT

                                                  Geometry-conditioned multi-view diffusion supplies images for 3D texturing, but use-
     Keywords: multi-view diffusion, 3D tex-      ful control strength depends on the implementation and evaluation endpoint. We study
     turing, geometry adapter, residual scal-     Timestep-Conditioned Adapter Scaling (TCAS) on an existing GeoTex-Adapter resid-
     ing, timestep conditioning                   ual path, retaining C3 as a historical middle-high candidate. A complete 300-object
                                                  equal-applied-scale-budget comparison finds that C3 trails fixed mean by 0.13 dB and
                                                  late-high by 0.56 dB FG-PSNR; fixed-high wins this endpoint on 172 objects and C3 on
                                                  none. These controlled allocation effects do not establish a residual-dose-independent
                                                  mechanism. On ten previously unbaked, compatibility-selected objects, calibrated
                                                  native-UV baking with eleven unseen views per object yields a Late-high–Fixed Mean
                                                  FG-LPIPS difference of −0.00265 (95% object-bootstrap CI [−0.00414, −0.00137]), fa-
                                                  vorable on all ten. C3’s PSNR advantage over Fixed High does not repeat stably and

                                                  ([−0.019, +0.081]), without a separated primary gain. Scheduling is established prior
                                                  work; we withdraw independent CAI derivation and optimality claims. The contribu-
                                                  tion is controlled, task-specific evidence, a small relative baked-image improvement
                                                  and explicit cross-interface limits, not a universally optimal schedule or validated PBR
