underlying rows and the full inventory remain in `NEGATIVE_RESULT_LEDGER.md`.

## Claim consequences

- The defensible story is bounded characterization of depth allocation and
  tested timing contrasts, not an empirically validated two-dimensional
  interaction law.
- The strongest competitor matters more than extremely small p-values. Report
  effect sizes, confidence intervals, and ratios to the retrospective
  realization-drift reference; do not convert statistical detection into
  practical importance.
- Human preference is needed to address the gap between scalar metrics and
  visible material fidelity. The frozen pair set already includes the
  endpoint-matched linear competitor and may not be altered after collection
  begins.
- MV-Adapter remains a boundary result. Do not change its grouping, windows,
  or scales to obtain a positive interaction.
- 3D evidence is now bounded to GLB-native base-color outcomes on 20
  UV-present objects. These endpoints cannot be collapsed into a unique
  quality score; the four no-UV objects remain outside scope.
