# Intermediate scientific decision after B unblinding — 2026-10-05

Update 2026-10-06: native base-color rendering of the stored N=20/8 GLBs is
complete. It shows mixed endpoints and no unique 3D winner; P1-9 is closed
for those stored outputs, while strict N=24 coverage and the human-fidelity

## Decision

Select provisional `NARRATIVE_D`: characterize adapter scaling and residual
allocation, with depth allocation primary and tested temporal contrasts
secondary on the primary backbone. This decision is made after the completed
B campaign was already unblinded. Later safeguards and analyses are
retrospective, not a restored blinded confirmation.

## Why the full interaction is not load-bearing

- A2's corrected cluster-Wald results show a strong Layer × Window map at
  native dose. The production bootstrap had zero power, and the Wald
  reanalysis reuses that discovery cohort.
- A3b's bounded intervention detects heterogeneity, but its calibration is
  materially unbalanced and has no three-layer common support (0/2,250 rows).
  It does not identify dose-independent non-additivity.
- The MV-Adapter analysis does not recover a corrected global interaction in
  the tested 98/99 cohort. Its different intervention geometry makes this a
  boundary comparison, not an isolated causal test of architecture.

Accordingly, show measured depth and timing dimensions through their named
contrasts. Do not claim that they are independent mechanisms, that the full
Layer × Time law is dose-independent, or that MV-Adapter proves a universal
architecture-dependent law.

## Why LLH is not selected as a universal winner

- LLH−LFM-EXACT is statistically detectable but inside the frozen practical
  margins; realized residual norms differ.
- The four-way early/late signature does not hold across endpoints. HLL−LLL
  is inconclusive on FG-PSNR and favors LLL on FG-LPIPS.
- Registered `gen_linear` is a credible primary-endpoint competitor. The
  registered C comparison has no detected LLH FG-PSNR advantage, without a
  formal equivalence margin. The B generic extension is post-lock, same-
  cohort sensitivity and is not independent confirmation.
- The realization-drift reference is from a prior 24-object, three-seed
  development probe. It was computed without B rows but recorded after B
  exposure; use it as scale context, not as a prospective threshold.

Do not retune LLH or run FRESH_CONFIRM_C to search for a winner. The current
evidence supports characterization, not schedule optimization.

3. Do not add a third backbone on current evidence. The feasibility matrix
   does not identify a candidate that meets its frozen gates; the MV-Adapter
