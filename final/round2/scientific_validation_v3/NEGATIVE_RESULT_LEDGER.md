# Negative-result ledger — Scientific Validation V3

This ledger preserves evidence that limits the method or the current
submission claims. Pending human outcomes are deliberately not guessed.

| Result | Claim effect | What it establishes | Future role / follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| A3 high-dose shallow intervention reached a reported residual multiplier of 30.38, with strong saturation/stress behavior. | **Narrows** the safe operating range; does not invalidate native-scale effects. | Treat the extreme dose as stress-test evidence, not a recommended operating point or dose-normalized mechanism result. | Preserve as a failure boundary; no high-dose retuning on observed cohorts. |
| A3b calibration was deep −20.01%, middle −20.20%, shallow +98.98%; its actual layer-dose ranges have no three-way overlap. | **Narrows** the Layer × Window claim. | Raw and post-dose-adjusted interaction tests are significant, but common-support identification fails; final classification is `PARTIAL`. | Report the bounded map and unresolved dose mediation; no replacement-map search on B. |
| MV-Adapter did not reproduce a corrected global interaction; only small/local shallow late-window FG-LPIPS changes survived the cell family. | **Invalidates** transfer of a broad universal interaction claim; does not prove no interaction for all MV-Adapter scales. | The evidence is bounded to the tested 0.75–1.00 intervention and single-window design. | State the architecture boundary; do not search scales, windows, or favorable objects. |
| `g_0098` was excluded because its frozen GLB loads as a path geometry without triangle faces; 98 of 99 planned objects were analyzed. | **Narrows** cohort scope; not an outcome-based exclusion. | One technical source-format limitation is documented with its hash; no replacement or conversion was used. | Keep the exclusion visible in methods and reproducibility records. |
