## Claims allowed in a future rewrite

- Native-dose A2 shows a strong discovery Layer × Window map on the primary
  backbone; this does not establish a dose-independent mechanism. The
  same-cohort Wald reanalysis is discovery-level, not independent
  confirmation.
- B supports a narrow registered mean LLH-vs-HLL placement contrast at equal
  requested high duration. The full four-way directional signature fails,
  actual residual doses differ, and HLL−LLL is exploratory.
- Depth allocation and temporal placement may be discussed as separately
  measured operational dimensions, with backbone and contrast scope explicit;
  do not imply statistical independence or a universal schedule law.
- Report the texture-rich-object applicability boundary with its limitations.
- Do not use the old CPU unseen metrics or seam values as faithful-GLB
  evidence. The stored N=20/eight-condition GLBs now have a separate
  GLB-native base-color evaluation with matching repeat/mipmap sampler in
  `GLB_NATIVE_EGL_RENDER_REPORT_20261006.md`. This bounds support to 20
  UV-present objects and does not establish full PBR or human material
  fidelity. The historical run summary still lists 16/128 although outputs
  and metrics cover 20/160; four UV-less objects remain excluded.

## Claims forbidden until new evidence

- A validated equal-dose or dose-independent full Layer × Time law.
- A universal early-high penalty, universal late-high benefit, or unique
  optimal schedule.
- Cross-backbone transfer of the layer/time response.
- Human-validated faithful material reproduction or full 24-object baked
  superiority.

4. Final manuscript tables, figures, reviewer-by-reviewer closure, a full
