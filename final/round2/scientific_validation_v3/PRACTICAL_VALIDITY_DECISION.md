# Practical validity decision — refreshed 2026-10-06

**Decision: bounded practical evidence; no unique schedule winner.** Image-space
effects vary by endpoint and object. The stored N=20/8 exported GLBs have now
been reevaluated with their embedded repeat/mipmap sampler semantics using an
unlit base-color renderer. Human material fidelity remains untested. See
`GLB_NATIVE_EGL_RENDER_REPORT_20261006.md` for hashes, code, endpoints, and
scope limits.

| Dimension | Current evidence and decision |
|---|---|
| Image-space reference fidelity | B is complete and integrity-checked. LLH−LFM-EXACT is statistically detectable but inside the frozen practical margins. The full LLH/HLL/LLL direction conjunction fails. Registered `gen_linear` remains a credible primary-endpoint competitor. Report absolute effects and intervals, not p-values alone. |
| Practical effect scale | The repeated-realization development drift in `PRACTICAL_EFFECT_REFERENCE.md` is a retrospective scale reference, not a threshold. B was unblinded before this reference and narrative safeguards were written. |
| Perceptual/material fidelity | The visual audit found color/material mismatch in 18/24 panels and fine-detail loss in 21/24. The locked 40-slot human study includes `gen_linear`, uses a fixed stop, and requires at least 36 valid completions. No responses or participant recruitment are recorded. |
| Texture richness | The prospective GT-only texture-boundary analysis replicates an association between texture complexity and schedule response. Treat it as an applicability boundary, not a per-object selection policy. |
| Baked 3D quality | The corrected renderer resolves sampler fidelity on stored GLBs for N=20 and eight conditions. LLH−`native_gfl` has FG-PSNR −0.422 dB [−1.361, +0.652], FG-LPIPS −0.01175 [−0.01988, −0.00564], and CIEDE2000 +2.563 [−1.007, +5.893]. This mixed endpoint pattern does not establish an overall 3D quality win. Four no-UV exclusions remain; results are base-color-only, not PBR or human evidence. |
| Cross-view seams | The old seam analyzer's clamp and extreme-coordinate integer-cast behavior invalidate its seam-specific values. Native unseen renders include the exported repeat addressing, but no causal seam mechanism or seam reduction claim is established. |
| Cross-backbone boundary | MV-Adapter remains scoped to 98/99 planned objects and the tested intervention geometry. The corrected global Layer × Window interaction is absent; do not search groupings or scales to manufacture a positive. |
| Failure cases | Color/material mismatch, fine-detail loss, texture-rich-object losses, and metric-dependent responses remain in scope. Preserve these cases in the paper. |

The stored bake textures were generated by the original clamp-based process;
they were rendered as exported, with no repeat-aware rebake. Strict all-24
3D coverage remains unsupported because four source objects had no UV layer.
The B narrative safeguards are retrospective. No FRESH_CONFIRM_C schedule
search or third-backbone run is indicated by these results. The human site is
ready for the locked study; participant recruitment and the CSV return route
remain to be arranged. The manuscript has not been edited.
