## Release rule

Codex C may use a row only after the status is `SUPPORTED` or an explicitly
qualified `SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITATIONS`, with the exact checkpoint, cohort,
metric provenance and scope copied into the paper table. G1–G4 now meet that
qualified release condition; the authorization is recorded in
`coordination/C_PAPER_EDIT_AUTHORIZATION.md`. `PENDING`,
`PROTOCOL_MISMATCH`, and `HISTORICAL_ONLY` rows are not paper evidence.
Rows marked `CASE-STUDY_ONLY` may support only implementation/feasibility
wording with the stated cohort and limitations; they cannot support a
headline superiority or population-generalization claim.
